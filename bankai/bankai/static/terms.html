BankAI — SMS Terms & Conditions

Last updated: July 29, 2026

1. The service

BankAI is a private, self-hosted household finance assistant. The SMS program lets the pre-authorized household members text questions about their own linked financial data and receive replies and self-configured account alerts. It is read-only: it cannot move money or transact on any account.

2. Eligibility and consent

The program is available exclusively to the household members whose phone numbers the account owners have added to the service configuration. By adding your number and texting the service (for example the keyword START, or any first message), you consent to receive conversational replies and the alerts you configure. There is no public enrollment.

3. Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on your usage and the alerts you configure. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier plan. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

4. Opting out and help

Reply STOP to cancel at any time; you will stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for assistance. Numbers can also be removed from the service configuration at any time.

5. No warranty

Information provided by the assistant is for the household's personal reference only and is not financial advice. The service is provided as-is, without warranty of any kind.

6. Privacy

See the accompanying SMS Privacy Policy. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
