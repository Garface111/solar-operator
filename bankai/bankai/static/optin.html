BankAI — SMS Opt-In Policy & Message Flow

Campaign collateral / proof of consent · Last updated: July 29, 2026

How end users opt in

BankAI is a private household assistant used exclusively by the two household account owners. There is no advertising, no public keyword campaign, and no way for anyone else to enroll:

  1. The account owners add their own two phone numbers to the service's private configuration. The service ignores every other number.
  2. A member opts in by texting the service — the keyword START or any first message. The first reply is a welcome message with the program disclosures (shown below).
  3. Message frequency varies: conversational replies to the member's own questions, plus account alerts the members configure themselves. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to opt out.
