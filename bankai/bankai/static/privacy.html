BankAI — SMS Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

BankAI is a private household finance assistant operated by its account owners for their own personal use. This policy covers the SMS messaging program associated with the BankAI phone number.

Who receives messages

Only the household members whose phone numbers are explicitly pre-authorized in the service configuration can send or receive messages. The service does not respond to, message, or solicit any other phone number. There is no public sign-up.

What we collect and how it is used

The program stores the pre-authorized phone numbers and the content of the SMS conversation in order to provide replies and requested account alerts. Messages are processed by the operator's self-hosted application and its SMS provider (Twilio) solely to deliver the service.

No sharing of mobile information

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the above categories exclude text messaging originator opt-in data and consent; this information will not be shared with any third parties.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies: the assistant replies conversationally when a household member texts it, plus occasional account alerts that members configure themselves (for example bill reminders or balance alerts). Message and data rates may apply.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. You can also remove your number from the service configuration to end all messaging.

Contact

Questions about this program can be directed to the household operator at the contact email configured for the service.
